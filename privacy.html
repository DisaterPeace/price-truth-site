Price Truth

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Price Truth is built so that comparing prices never means giving up your privacy. We do not sell your data, we do not build advertising profiles, and we never attach your identity to anything. This page explains exactly what the extension does with data.

What we collect

When you open a product page on a supported store, Price Truth records the product’s price, product code, brand, barcode, title, the store, and the time, and sends this anonymously to our shared price database. This is what makes cross-store comparison possible — every price checked improves the data for everyone.

This is never linked to you. No account. No name, email, or sign-in. No user or device identifier is ever attached to it. No browsing profile is built. Web addresses are stripped of tracking parameters and reduced to the product path before they leave your device.

It is a catalogue of product prices, not a record of who looked at them. You can turn this contribution off in the extension’s settings; the extension still works using only what you’ve seen yourself.

What stays on your device

Your alerts, settings, and local price history stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere — except to check the price of a product you explicitly asked us to watch. By default we keep a price history only for products you set an alert on; opening a page otherwise stores nothing.

Permissions

  • Storage & alarms — to save your settings and alerts and check watched prices periodically.
  • Store websites — the extension can read product pages only on the stores in the countries you enable, and only to read prices. It requests access to those stores when you enable a country, and nothing before.
  • Notifications (optional) — requested only when you create your first price alert.

No access to your browsing history, no access to sites other than the stores you enable, no cookies, no keystroke or form data.

How we make money

If you click a link to buy at another store, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only ever add a referral when you click a button — never just because you visited a page — and if that link already credits someone else, we take nothing. Every commissioned link is labelled where you click it.

Data sharing

Anonymous product prices are pooled across everyone using Price Truth so that one shopper’s find helps the next. We do not sell personal data, and there is no personal data in the price pool to sell — it contains no identifiers.

Your choices

  • Turn off anonymous price contribution in settings.
  • Delete all local data (alerts, settings, history) with one button in settings.
  • Disable any country to revoke the extension’s access to those stores.
